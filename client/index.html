IronPath

A free workout tracker that works entirely offline. No account, no signup, no server — your training log stays on your phone.

  • Plan a rotation on a calendar, or let it schedule itself
  • Log weight, reps and rest set by set, prefilled from last time
  • Build custom workouts and add your own exercises
  • Works with no signal at all, and installs like an app
  • Export a full backup whenever you want

IronPath needs JavaScript to run. It is free and open source — source on GitHub.
